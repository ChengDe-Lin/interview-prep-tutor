# Team Fit

## HR 想確認什麼

你能不能補足團隊：rigor / grit / technical strength / regional expertise。這題可以把你的快速成長、跨團隊影響、高層溝通、語言能力放進來。

這幾個詞的意思：

- `rigor`：嚴謹度。做事有邏輯、有品質標準，不是只求快。可以用 Code Champion、schema automation、spec-driven development 來支撐。
- `grit`：韌性。遇到困難、阻力、長期推動的事情，能撐住並把事情做完。可以用 micro-frontend migration、AI seminar、agentic workflow adoption 來支撐。
- `technical strength`：技術深度。真的 build 過東西，不只是會講 AI。
- `regional expertise`：區域適配。Mandarin + English、台灣/新加坡經驗、APJ 溝通能力。

## SA 角度

這題不要只說自己好相處。要講你能帶來什麼：

- technical strength / learning velocity：Code Champion、不到其他同事一半時間升 Senior、manager 已討論年底更高 level path。
- collaboration：Peer Feedback Award，同事認可你是合作起來舒服、可靠、有影響力的人。
- executive communication：目前組織中唯一非 manager、會代表部門向副董事長報告技術內容的人。
- communication / influence：因為溝通表達和組織能力，被交付主辦 AI seminar。
- rigor：工程品質、spec-driven development、schema automation、code quality。
- grit：micro-frontend migration、AI seminar review、agentic workflow adoption 都是長期推動，不是一次 demo。
- APJ / regional fit：Mandarin + English，理解台灣/新加坡/醫療/企業場景。

## Opening STAR 口說稿

> I think I can complement the team in four ways.
>
> First, I am a fast learner with strong technical depth. I was promoted to Senior Engineer in less than half of the usual time in my organization, and my manager has also started discussing a higher-level path for me by the end of the year. I do not treat that as guaranteed, but I see it as a signal that the organization recognizes my learning speed and the impact I can create beyond individual delivery.
>
> Technically, I have built agentic workflows, multiple AI tools, and large workflow transformation projects. So I am not only interested in AI at a concept level. I have seen the practical problems around adoption, governance, testing, and production reliability.
>
> I also care a lot about engineering rigor. One signal is that I received a department Code Champion award, which was meaningful to me because it reflected not just delivery speed, but code quality and technical judgment recognized by the team.
>
> Second, I think I am strong in collaboration. I received a Peer Feedback Award from my colleagues, which means a lot to me because it came from people who worked with me directly. I think they recognized that I am not only technical, but also someone who is easy to work with, willing to help, and able to create positive cross-team impact.
>
> Third, I am comfortable communicating across different levels. In my current organization, I am the only non-manager who regularly represents the department to present technical topics to our vice chairman. I think that shows the trust the organization has in my ability to explain technical work clearly to senior stakeholders.
>
> I was also trusted to host the AI seminar series for over 100 people in the department, because of my communication and organizing ability. I was not join the seminar as the speaker myself. A big part of the work was finding the right speakers, convincing them to share, let them this has huge potentail in the future, shaping the content with them, and keeping the whole program moving.
>
> Fourth, I think I fit well with an APJ-facing role. I can work in both Mandarin and English, and I have experience in Taiwan and Singapore. I am used to explaining technical ideas across different cultures and levels of technical depth.
>
> So the value I hope to bring is a mix of technical depth, rigor, collaboration, executive communication, and the ability to drive adoption across teams.
